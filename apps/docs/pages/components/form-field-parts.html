Label, hint and error

The text that names a control, says what it needs and says what went wrong.

Letters, numbers and dashes.
Error: Use letters, numbers and dashes only.
Code Design

Code

Copy the markup

.luz-field wraps the label, the control, the hint and the error. aria-describedby ties the hint and the error to the control:

HTML 
<div class="luz-field">
  <label class="luz-field__label luz-field__label--required" for="slug">Slug</label>
  <input class="luz-input luz-input--error" id="slug" type="text" required aria-invalid="true" aria-describedby="slug-hint slug-error">
  <div class="luz-field__helper" id="slug-hint">Letters, numbers and dashes.</div>
  <div class="luz-field__error" id="slug-error"><span class="u-sr-only">Error: </span>Use letters, numbers and dashes only.</div>
</div>

Variants and options

  • .luz-field__label: the visible label. --required adds a red " *" after it.
  • .luz-field__helper: the hint, under the control. .luz-field__error: the error, in the danger colour.
  • .luz-input--error with aria-invalid="true": a 2 px danger underline on the control.

How it works

The label names the control through for and id, and a click on it focuses the control. LUZ does not mark a field invalid for you: add the error class, aria-invalid and the message when your validation fails.

Without JavaScript

The label, the hint and the error are text in your HTML, so they show with no script. The browser still checks required and the type when the form is sent.

Keyboard and ARIA

  • A label and a message take no focus and have no keys of their own.
  • Write "Error: " before the error text, hidden with .u-sr-only if you like, and list the hint and the error in aria-describedby.

Contrast and focus are in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use a label on every control, a hint when the reader needs the format or the reason, and an error after a check fails.

  • The label always shows: it stays on screen while the reader types.
  • The error goes beside the field it is about, in words.

When not to use

Do not swap one of these for another, and do not hide any of them.

  • A placeholder is not a label: it disappears when the reader types.
  • A colour or a border alone is not an error: say what is wrong. Do not show the error only in a toast: it goes away.

Do and don't

Writing the content

  • The label is a short noun in sentence case: "Project name".
  • The hint says the format or why you ask.
  • The error says what is wrong and how to fix it. If you mark required fields with "*", say once, above the form, what it means.

Fieldset

A bordered group of related controls, named by its legend.

Shipping address
Code Design

Code

Copy the markup

A native <fieldset> whose first child is a <legend>:

HTML 
<fieldset class="luz-fieldset">
  <legend>Shipping address</legend>
  <div class="luz-field">
    <label class="luz-field__label" for="street">Street</label>
    <input class="luz-input" id="street" type="text">
  </div>
  <div class="luz-field">
    <label class="luz-field__label" for="city">City</label>
    <input class="luz-input" id="city" type="text">
  </div>
</fieldset>

Variants and options

  • disabled on the fieldset disables every control inside it.
  • role="group" with aria-labelledby on a <div> names a group with no border, as the checkbox example does.

How it works

A native <fieldset> styled by components/form.css: a 1 px border, soft corners and the field background. The <legend> is small upper-case text.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes: the fieldset and its legend are native and need no script.

Keyboard and ARIA

  • A fieldset takes no focus; Tab moves through the controls inside it.
  • The legend names the group: the browser exposes a fieldset as a group named by its legend.

Contrast and focus are in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use a fieldset for controls that answer one question together.

  • A set of radio buttons or checkboxes, or an address.
  • Put the legend first and keep it short.

When not to use

A fieldset is a group with a name, not a box for layout.

  • A single control: its label is enough.
  • Spacing or columns: the layout objects, o-stack or o-grid. Do not wrap every section of a form.

Do and don't

Writing the content

  • The legend is a noun or a question: "Shipping address", "Plan".
  • No colon at the end: the style adds the space.
  • Do not repeat the legend in each label.
