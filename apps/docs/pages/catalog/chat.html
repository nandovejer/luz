Example

Experimental. A conversation in the style of a messaging app: tight bubbles, a time beside what was said, and the writer's name for a screen reader. It does not know who answers; this example has a provider of the page's own, with canned answers.

The chat needs JavaScript. Read the documentation.

Known issues

  • It is experimental: its provider contract can change in a minor release.
  • It has not been tested with a screen reader.
