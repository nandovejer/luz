Example

Three places, each with an icon and its name; then two as icons alone, where the name is read aloud but not shown.

  • GitHub
  • Mastodon
  • RSS feed
  • GitHub

LUZ ships no brand logos: the name says which place. The icons are those of the Icons page, or yours.

How it works

CSS from components/social.css of @luz-ds/catalog. No JavaScript.

  • An svg with a viewBox and its paths is enough. In a footer, link it after site-footer.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes.

Keyboard and ARIA

The links are native.

  • The icon is aria-hidden: the text, shown or in .u-sr-only, names the link.
  • Wrap it in <nav aria-label="…"> only if readers look for it alone.
  • Targets are 36 px (icons alone) or 28 px high, over WCAG 2.5.8's 24 px.

Contrast and focus are in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use them to send a reader to your profiles and feeds: in the foot of a site, under an author's name or on a contact page.

When not to use

Do not use them for the pages of your own site or to share the current page.

  • For the site's links, use site-footer or the navbar.

Do and don't

Writing the content

The name is the place as it calls itself, in one or two words. The link goes to your profile there, not to its home page.

  • Write “Mastodon”, “GitHub”, “RSS feed”, “Email”: a noun, not “Follow us on…”.
  • Give an address as a mailto: link and call it “Email”.
  • Keep to six links at most; more is a list readers skip.
