Known issues

What to expect:

  • The script for the head ignores latitude and longitude: with a place, the element corrects the moment after the first paint, so it can change once near sunrise or sunset.
  • The moments are written twice: in the markup and in data-moments of the script for the head. Nothing checks that they agree, so change both together.
  • Without data-luz-label-scrub the slider has no name, and without data-luz-label-saved the note is silent: no English fallback.
  • Only the first <luz-theme-time> with moments owns the page.
  • It has not been tested with a screen reader.
Code Design

Code

Copy the markup

Wrap core's <luz-theme-mode> in <luz-theme-time> and add the moments of the day after it, one radio each, in the order of the day. Its value is the name of the moment; its label is what a reader sees.

HTML 
<script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@luz-ds/core@0.2.1/dist/luz.min.js"></script>
<script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@luz-ds/catalog@0.2.1/dist/catalog.min.js"></script>

<luz-theme-time>
  <luz-theme-mode>
    …the fieldset and the four radios of core's page…
  </luz-theme-mode>
  <fieldset class="luz-theme-time__moments">
    <legend class="luz-theme-time__legend">Time of day</legend>
    <label class="luz-theme-time__moment"><input class="luz-theme-time__input" type="radio" name="luz-theme-moment" value="dawn" data-from="05:00" data-theme="dark">Dawn</label>
    <label class="luz-theme-time__moment"><input class="luz-theme-time__input" type="radio" name="luz-theme-moment" value="morning" data-from="07:30" data-theme="light">Morning</label>
    <label class="luz-theme-time__moment"><input class="luz-theme-time__input" type="radio" name="luz-theme-moment" value="afternoon" data-from="12:00" data-theme="light" data-default>Afternoon</label>
    <label class="luz-theme-time__moment"><input class="luz-theme-time__input" type="radio" name="luz-theme-moment" value="sunset" data-from="17:30" data-theme="light">Sunset</label>
    <label class="luz-theme-time__moment"><input class="luz-theme-time__input" type="radio" name="luz-theme-moment" value="dusk" data-from="20:30" data-theme="dark">Dusk</label>
    <label class="luz-theme-time__moment"><input class="luz-theme-time__input" type="radio" name="luz-theme-moment" value="night" data-from="22:30" data-theme="dark" data-default>Night</label>
  </fieldset>
</luz-theme-time>

catalog.min.js loads theme-time.min.js when the browser is idle and the page has the element. To load it on its own, import @luz-ds/catalog/theme-time and link components/theme-time.css after core's.

Variants and options

A bar with a place, and the texts it needs:

HTML 
<luz-theme-time variant="bar" show="time place saved" place="Madrid" latitude="40.42" longitude="-3.7"
                 data-luz-label-scrub="Hour of the day" data-luz-label-saved="Saved">
  <luz-theme-mode inline>
    …the fieldset and the four radios of core's page…
  </luz-theme-mode>
</luz-theme-time>
Class or attributeEffect
variant="bar"
Adds the slider of the 24 hours: by default the slider alone.
show
Words that add parts to the bar: time (the hour in large type), place (the place and the moment), saved (the note “Saved”; without it the note is still written, for a screen reader) and moments (the declared moments as options under the slider; choosing one pins its hour).
place
The name of the place for show="place"; a label, it does not set the sun.
latitude, longitude
Degrees north and east of the place whose sunrise and sunset set the turns of the day.
no-remember
A pinned hour is not stored under luz-light.
On a moment: value, data-from, data-theme, data-default
Its name (written to data-moment); where it starts, HH:MM by the visitor's clock; the base it is painted over, light or dark; and the one a chosen mode shows (light, the default light moment; dark and high contrast, the default dark one).
data-luz-label-scrub, data-luz-label-saved
On the element or an ancestor: the slider's name and the note “Saved”.

Properties, methods and event

NameWhat it does
el.hour, el.moments, el.moment, el.pinned, el.place
Read-only: the hour in use (0 to 24); the declared moments, { name, from, theme, input }; the name of the moment shown and the pinned hour (each null if none); the place attribute.
preview(hour), pin(hour), unpin()
The page follows that hour, in Auto, and nothing is stored (preview(null) goes back); Auto at that hour instead of now, stored under luz-light unless no-remember; lets go of the pinned hour.
luz-theme-time:change
Bubbles after the upgrade and when the moment or the pinned hour changes. detail: moment and pinned (each null if none).

A method returns false when it does nothing: no moments, or an hour outside 0 to 24.

How it works

theme-time.js gives Auto of <luz-theme-mode> the hour. Core does the rest, and the button alone if the catalog does not load.

  • Auto by the clock: the mode is the base of the moment now, by the visitor's local hour, checked every minute. The moment now is the last one that has started (night runs through midnight): its name goes to data-moment and its radio is checked. The visitor's location is never asked.
  • A mode the reader chose shows its default moment. High contrast always wins: data-theme is high-contrast, so a rule for "light" or "dark" does not match.
  • With a place, latitude and longitude give today's real sunrise and sunset, worked out in the page with no network, in place of the two turns of the day; if the sun does not rise or set there, the declared hours stand.
  • A pinned hour is Auto at that hour, stored under luz-light to five minutes; choosing a mode, or Auto, in <luz-theme-mode> lets it go.
  • The slider (variant="bar"): dragging previews the hour; letting go pins and stores it and writes the note “Saved”. Its track is painted with --luz-theme-time-sky-<value>, one for each moment: Paint the track.
  • Two files: theme-time.min.js and theme-time-bar.min.js, which only variant="bar" loads. For a bar added after the scan, import @luz-ds/catalog/theme-time-bar and call initThemeTimeBar(root).

Without JavaScript

There is no bar and the moments are not shown. Core's radios still change the mode; Auto is light, and data-moment is not written.

Keyboard and ARIA

  • Slider: a native range in steps of 5 minutes: Tab reaches it, the arrow keys move one step, Home goes to 00:00 and End to 23:55. Each press previews the hour.
  • Escape on the slider lets go of a pinned hour, and then does not close a dialog around the bar.
  • Name and value: the name is data-luz-label-scrub; aria-valuetext is the moment and the hour, “Afternoon, 13:00”. When an hour is pinned a role="status" region says your data-luz-label-saved, shown or not.
  • Moments as options (show="moments") are radios in a fieldset: the arrow keys choose one. The mode button is core's: its keyboard and ARIA.

Script for the head

Optional, after core's. It writes the moment before the first paint. Copy node_modules/@luz-ds/catalog/dist/theme-time-boot.min.js to your own origin. The moments travel on its tag, as data-moments: the body is not read yet.

HTML 
<head>
  <script src="/theme-boot.min.js"></script>
  <script src="/theme-time-boot.min.js"
          data-moments="dawn@05:00=dark morning@07:30=light afternoon@12:00=light* sunset@17:30=light dusk@20:30=dark night@22:30=dark*"></script>
  <link rel="stylesheet" href="…">
</head>
  • data-moments: value@HH:MM=base for each moment, in markup order; * marks data-default..
  • What it writes: data-moment on <html> and, in Auto, data-mode (and data-theme, with no brand) of that moment's base. A stored mode shows its default moment; a pinned hour (luz-light) replaces the clock; prefers-contrast: more wins. It never writes the storage. It blocks rendering as core's does, under the same exception (EX-012).

Paint each moment

The colours are yours: set only the tokens that differ, over the base the moment names, as a theme of your own does, and measure each pair with the contrast check.

CSS 
/* Painted over the base the moment names: only the tokens that differ */
:root[data-theme="light"][data-moment="morning"] {
  --luz-surface-page: #fdf3dc;
  --luz-surface: #fffaf0;
}

:root[data-theme="dark"][data-moment="dawn"] {
  --luz-surface-page: #0f1520;
  --luz-surface: #172030;
}

/* With a brand of your own: */
:root[data-theme="my-brand"][data-mode="light"][data-moment="morning"] { … }

Paint the track

Set one property for each moment, once, on :root:

CSS 
:root {
  --luz-theme-time-sky-night: #171b36;
  --luz-theme-time-sky-dawn: #6b3f5f;
  --luz-theme-time-sky-afternoon: #cfe1f6;
}
  • Without them, a bar gets a neutral ramp of tokens. It reads which properties are set when it is built; the colours stay variables, so a mode that changes them repaints the track.
  • High contrast: under :root[data-theme="high-contrast"] set every moment to var(--luz-text-primary): one solid fill that keeps its contrast. In forced colors the track is CanvasText. In right to left the day runs from the right.
PropertyEffect
--luz-theme-time-sky-<value>
Yours, on :root: the colour of a moment on the track.
--luz-theme-time-sky
Written by the bar: the gradient of the track. Set it for a track of your own.
--luz-theme-time-edge
The ring around the track, a box-shadow (default --luz-text-secondary); keep 3:1 against the page.

Design

When to use

Use it when a site has a look for each part of the day, or when readers should be able to try the light at another hour.

  • The moments alone, in a footer or a navbar, for a site that changes its look with the day.
  • The bar, where the light is part of the page, such as a home page.

When not to use

Do not use it when the site has one look in one mode: core's color mode is enough.

  • Do not promise the real sunrise without a place: the declared hours are fixed.
  • Do not use the bar as the only control: keep core's button.
  • Do not ask for the visitor's location: write the place yourself.

Do and don't

Writing the content

Call the moments by the light they show: “Dawn”, “Morning”, “Afternoon”, “Sunset”, “Dusk” and “Night”.

  • The labels are in the language of the page: the slider reads them with the hour.
  • Name the slider for what it does, “Hour of the day”, and the note “Saved”.
  • Say near the control what Auto follows: “Auto follows the time of day”.

  • Focus: a 2 px outline, 2 px outside the slider or the moment, in --luz-focus-ring.
  • Track against the page: the ring uses --luz-text-secondary on --luz-surface-page, the pair in the table. Read on the pixels of the worst stretch of the track, which is a gradient and a soft edge the table cannot measure, the implementer found 3.48:1 in light, 4.63:1 in dark and 21:1 in high contrast.
  • Not colour alone: the track has a soft ring in --luz-text-secondary around its gradient, the hour is written as text, and by night the thumb is a crescent. In forced colors the track is CanvasText with a 1 px outline of the same colour, and the checked moment is a system highlight.
  • Target: the slider is 2.75rem high over a track 6 px high: the target is the slider, the track only draws.
  • Motion: the note fades in and out; there is no other motion.
  • Print: the element is not printed.
