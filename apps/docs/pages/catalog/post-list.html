Code Design

Code

Copy the markup

A list, one card in each item. The first item of the lead list has its text in a <div> beside the picture:

HTML 
<ul class="luz-post-list luz-post-list--lead" role="list">
  <li>
    <article class="luz-card">
      <div class="luz-image luz-image--3x2"><img src="/media/tide.jpg" width="600" height="400" alt="" loading="lazy"></div>
      <div>
        <p class="luz-tag luz-tag--gray u-mb-sm">Coast</p>
        <h2 class="luz-card__title"><a href="/2026/10/tide-records/">What ten years of tide records say about the bay</a></h2>
        <p class="luz-card__body">Volunteers have read the gauge in the port twice a day since 2016.</p>
        <p class="luz-meta"><time datetime="2026-10-12">12 October 2026</time> <span>6 min read</span></p>
      </div>
    </article>
  </li>
  <!-- one li for each post; the others have no div around their text -->
</ul>

Variants and options

ClassEffect
.luz-post-list
On a <ul>: as many equal columns as fit, none under --luz-post-list-min (16rem), no bullets. Each card is as tall as the tallest in its row
.luz-post-list--lead
From 40rem of list width, the first item spans two columns and its card puts the picture beside the text, with a larger title
.luz-post-list--compact
One column, a row for each post: the title, and the facts at its end when there is room. Leave out the picture and the summary
--luz-post-list-min
The narrowest a column may be. Set it on the list or an ancestor

How it works

CSS from components/post-list.css of @luz-ds/catalog. It sets the layout; the post is core's card, with the picture, tag, title, summary and meta line you choose.

  • The list is its own size container, so the lead follows the room it has and not the window. Give it a parent that sets its width: a flex or grid item sized by its content leaves it no width.
  • Under 40rem the lead is a card like the rest, with its picture above.
  • The link on the title makes the whole card one link, as in any card.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes. It is a list of cards, and the package has no script.

Keyboard and ARIA

  • Order: the source order, which is the reading order. In the lead the picture comes before the text; each card is one tab stop, on its title link.
  • List: the <ul> is announced with its count ("list, 12 items"). Keep role="list": some browsers drop the list from a <ul> without bullets.
  • Names: each link is named by its post title. A picture that only decorates the post has alt="". Give the list a heading, or an aria-label, when the page has more than one.

Contrast and focus are in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use it whenever a page shows several posts, so every list on the site is laid out alike.

  • Base: the posts of a category or a tag.
  • Lead: the home, when one post should be read first.
  • Compact: an archive by date, or the results of a search.

When not to use

Do not use it for a single card, or for items that are not posts.

  • One post is a card. Products, people and plans are cards in a .o-grid--auto.
  • For the pages of a long list, add pagination after it.

Do and don't

Writing the content

  • Title: the title of the post, as on the post. Wrap, never cut.
  • Summary: one sentence, up to about 120 characters. Leave it out in the lead's side posts and in the compact list.
  • Facts: the date, as a <time>, then the reading time or the section. Same order in every card.
  • Heading level: the card title is a heading one level under the heading of the list's page or section.
