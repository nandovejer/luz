Paste the prompt into any LLM. It asks about your blog and your CMS in short rounds, shows you the plan and writes nothing until you say "go".

Open the prompt

Or build it yourself from the recipes below. It works with any CMS or static site generator: each recipe is HTML for one of its templates, and the CMS fills in the text. They use the classes of core, the catalog and cms only; Install LUZ lists the files to load.

Header

The skip link, the header with the name of the blog and its sections, the <main> and the footer. For a newspaper, the pattern has a masthead that does not stick. The search and the language switch are the catalog's elements: leave them out if you do not load it.

Post card

One post in a list: a picture, its section, the title, a line of summary and the date. It is core's card, with no CSS of its own. The link on the title makes the whole card one link, with one tab stop; the section is a tag, not a second link.

A list of posts is a <ul> on .o-grid--auto, which fits columns of 16rem or more. More cards, the related posts and the author box are in CMS templates.

Table of contents

The sections of a long story, beside its text: the table of contents, in an <article> with the text as core's prose. Leave it out of a short post, and the layout is the text alone.

Contents

  1. How the readings were taken
  2. Four centimetres in ten years

Since March 2016, a group of volunteers has read the tide gauge in the port at six in the morning and six in the evening.

How the readings were taken

Each reading is the height of the water against the gauge, to the nearest centimetre, with the time and the wind.

Four centimetres in ten years

The average height at high tide was 4 cm higher in 2025 than in 2016.

Beside the text when the article is 46rem wide or more, above it below that. With the catalog's script, the section in view is marked as you scroll.
