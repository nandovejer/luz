Paste the prompt into any LLM. It asks about your application in short rounds, shows you the plan and writes nothing until you say "go".

Open the prompt

Or build it yourself from the recipes below. They use the classes of core, the catalog and app only; Install LUZ lists the files to load. The view's <h1> and its main action are in the dashboard, never in the bar: the bar holds what is the same in every view.

Shell with a sidebar

The whole page: the skip link, then core's sidebar beside .o-main, which holds the header and the view. .o-shell puts the sidebar in a column from 769 px of window, and makes it a drawer that the menu button opens below that, with no script. The App patterns go inside it.

The shell is the whole page, so it has no example inside this one: this page is built on it. Make the window narrower than 769 px and the sidebar becomes a drawer behind the menu button.

Core's script, if you load it, marks the current link of the sidebar and closes the drawer when a link is followed.

HTML 
<body>
  <a class="luz-skip-link" href="#main-content">Skip to content</a>
  <div class="o-shell">
    <nav class="luz-sidebar" id="app-nav" aria-label="Main" popover>
      <a class="luz-sidebar__brand" href="/"><span class="luz-sidebar__brand-name">Bodega</span></a>
      <div class="luz-sidebar__section">
        <a class="luz-sidebar__link" href="/" aria-current="page">Overview</a>
        <a class="luz-sidebar__link" href="/reports/">Reports</a>
        <a class="luz-sidebar__link" href="/stock/">Stock</a>
      </div>
    </nav>
    <div class="o-main">
      <header class="luz-app-header o-sticky">…</header>
      <main class="luz-app-dashboard" id="main-content" tabindex="-1">…</main>
    </div>
  </div>
</body>

Header

The bar at the top of every view: the menu button, the name of the product and the account. It is the app header, in .o-main beside the sidebar. The search is the catalog's <luz-search>: leave it out if you do not load the catalog.

Bodega

  • Account settings
  • Sign out

Beside a sidebar the product name is the sidebar's, so a wide bar shows the account alone; at 768 px of window and below the name shows beside the menu button, which this example leaves out, as it has no sidebar to open. The account list opens under its button: press it.

Dashboard

A view: a title row with the view's <h1> and its actions, then panels in a grid that answers to the room of the view, not the window's. It is the dashboard, on the <main>. Each panel is a card that starts with its heading; the most important one can take the room of two columns.

The title is a paragraph and the panels' titles are <h3> here, as this page has its own <h1>.

Empty view

A view with nothing in it yet, on the dashboard: one wide panel that says what is missing and what to do, with the action. The title row keeps its place, so the view does not jump when the first panel arrives. The action in the panel is the view's one primary action: leave it out of the title row while the panel holds it.
