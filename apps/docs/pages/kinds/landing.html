Header

The skip link, a site header that stays at the top, the <main> that holds the blocks below, and the footer. .o-page makes the body a column at least as tall as the window, so the footer rests on the bottom of a short page.

Hero

The title of the page, one sentence, the main action and a picture: the hero. The page's one <h1> is here. The text comes first in the source, so it comes first for a screen reader and when the columns stack.

Give the image its real width and height, and an alt that says what it shows. Do not lazy-load it: it is the first thing on the page.

Section

One block of the page under its own <h2>: a section with a head (an eyebrow, a title and a lead) and features of equal weight as cards in a list. As a list, it tells a screen reader how many there are. .o-grid--split gives two columns when each gets 16rem, and one below.

For a call to action near the end of the page, add core's .luz-band to the section: the tint runs from edge to edge, and the text keeps the column.

Stats

Three figures that back the page's promise, each a stat with a short label under it, in a list on core's grid. Say where a figure comes from in the label or the lead, and keep it a figure a visitor can check.
