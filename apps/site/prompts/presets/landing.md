## The kind of site: a landing page

A landing page is one page, or a few, that presents one thing (a product, an event, a person) and leads the visitor to one action. Second round:

1. The sections, in reading order. Offer: hero, features, stats, programme, speakers, pricing or tickets, testimonials, call to action, sign-up form, footer. The hero and the footer are recommended.
2. The one main action: what the button says and where it goes.
3. The header: the name only, or the name with a few links and the colour mode switcher.
4. Any form, and where its data goes. LUZ styles the form; it does not send it.

Start from `luz-site-header`, `luz-hero`, `luz-main`, `luz-section`, `luz-stat` and `luz-site-footer`, on core's `o-container`, `o-stack` and `o-grid`. The cards, buttons and forms are core's.
