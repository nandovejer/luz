The look of `<luz-faq>`: the question is semibold in the body face, at the size of its answer (`--luz-text-lg`); the group title is centred, in capitals and smaller than the answer (no capitals in Arabic); a question under the pointer takes the colour of an open one and is not underlined; and long words break at any point (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`).
