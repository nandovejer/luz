Two pieces the home page had on its own are now in core. `luz-terminal` is a framed command: its tabs are the title bar and the Copy button sits at the end of the line it copies (`components/terminal.css`; `highlight.js` adds no second Copy inside a terminal). `luz-tabs--pill` is a variant of tabs for tabs that hold an icon, with the open one as a chip instead of a rule.
